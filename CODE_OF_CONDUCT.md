# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on ideas, implementation and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Public or private harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publishing private information without consent, and other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional open-source community are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Maintainers will review reports and may remove content, restrict participation or take other appropriate action.

This policy applies to project spaces and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.
